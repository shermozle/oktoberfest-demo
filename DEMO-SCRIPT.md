# Laneway Bank × Amplitude × Braze: Tom's run sheet

The run sheet is published at
**<https://shermozle.github.io/oktoberfest-demo/run-sheet/>**.

Its source is [src/run-sheet.html](src/run-sheet.html); edit that and
rebuild (`node build.mjs`) to change it. It has the tabs to open, the
set-up steps, what to click and say in each beat, the handover to Akshin
and back, and what's still to do before the demo.

The background runbook (data, cohort, Braze set-up) is [DEMO.md](DEMO.md).
